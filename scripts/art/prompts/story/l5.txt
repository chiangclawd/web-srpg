SCENE: Crimson Pass, a narrow mountain pass full of blazing red maple trees, falling red leaves. Image 1 is Morden, a huge barbarian warlord with wild orange-red hair, a leather vest, a fang necklace and a big sword. Image 2 is Arthur, a young blond knight in silver armor and a white cape.
