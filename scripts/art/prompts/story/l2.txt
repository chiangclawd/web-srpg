SCENE: A misty river ford in a frosty pine forest at early morning. Image 1 is Elena, an archer with long auburn hair tied with a green ribbon, green leaf armor and a longbow. Image 2 is Arthur, a young blond knight in silver armor and a white cape. Elena kneels at the muddy riverbank examining huge wolf paw prints in the light snow; Arthur stands behind her on a wooden bridge, hand on his sword. Deep in the dark trees across the river, pairs of pale glowing wolf eyes watch them.
