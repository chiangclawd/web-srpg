SCENE: A peaceful border village at dusk suddenly on alert. Image 1 is Gary, a weathered village guard captain in chainmail with steel shoulder plates and a spear. Image 2 is Arthur, a young blond knight in silver armor with a gold lion crest and a white cape lined with blue. Gary runs up the dirt road to Arthur at the wooden village gate, pointing back at the northern hills where several black columns of signal smoke rise into an orange sky. Behind them villagers load carts and lead children and a cow away; thatched cottages, a wooden palisade, warm lantern light.
