Story illustration for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9. The reactor core of a colossal starship, a chamber as vast as a cathedral (the place shown in the attached background).

In the centre a towering column of pale cyan-white light rises from the floor to the ceiling. All around it, on ring after ring of galleries receding into the dark, stand countless dark machines, each with a single red eye: hundreds of red points of light, and every one of them is turned toward the same small spot.

That spot is at the end of a narrow walkway at the foot of the column of light: a small control console, and in front of it the tiny white egg-shaped drone with two antenna ears and one orange stripe, floating, seen from behind and slightly to the side. Its cyan glow reflects on the console. It faces the light and the wall of red eyes alone.

At the near end of the walkway, in the middle of the picture and seen from behind, five mechs stand in a half-ring facing outward with their weapons raised, guarding the way to the drone: the white-and-orange mech, the yellow mech, the navy sniper mech, the slim red scout mech and a white command mech with crimson and gold trim.

Mood: the quiet before a last stand; one small voice in front of a choir.
