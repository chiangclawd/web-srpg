The unit: "Breacher", the close-assault frame of the Directorate army. Bipedal humanoid mech, leaner and more aggressive than a standard soldier frame: narrow waist, forward-leaning chest armor, large angular shoulder guards, a compact thruster pack on the back. BOTH forearms end in heavy armored gauntlets, each with a short wide breaching blade built along the outside of the forearm. Helmet head with a narrow V-shaped red visor and two short swept-back fins. Deep navy blue with crimson shoulder guards and thin gold trim, dark joints. Fast, brutal, made to break doors.
