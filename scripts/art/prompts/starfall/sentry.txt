The unit: "Sentry", an unmanned maintenance android repurposed as a soldier by a rogue ship AI. Bipedal humanoid robot with no cockpit, gaunt and slightly hunched: narrow torso, long arms with heavy forearms, a smooth featureless helmet head with ONE round glowing red eye. Dark gunmetal and charcoal armor with thin glowing teal lines along the limbs. A pulse gun barrel built into the right forearm. Cold, uncanny, slightly sinister but simple and readable.
