The unit: "Tribunal", the personal command walker of the Marshal of the Directorate army and the largest machine they own. Bipedal humanoid mech, towering and imposing, like a judge in armor: a broad chest with a tall armored collar rising behind the head, long layered armor skirts hanging from the waist like a robe, huge squared shoulders each topped with a boxy missile pod, and a very heavy long beam cannon built into the right forearm. The left forearm carries a tall narrow shield plate fixed along it. Helmet head with a vertical golden crest and a narrow red visor. Colors: black and deep navy armor with white chest and skirt panels, crimson lining and bold gold trim. Authoritarian, ceremonial, overwhelming.
