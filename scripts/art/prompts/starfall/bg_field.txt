Background painting for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9, seen from a high vantage point. A vast frozen plain on an ice moon just before dawn. On the left, far away, the enormous dark hull of a wrecked colony starship lies half buried in the ice, a few warm yellow lights in its breaches. On the right, far away on the horizon, a line of military dropships has landed: tiny crimson and white running lights, thin searchlight beams sweeping the sky, columns of exhaust steam. A huge ringed gas giant fills part of the sky, with a pale green aurora and the first thin band of orange dawn on the horizon.
