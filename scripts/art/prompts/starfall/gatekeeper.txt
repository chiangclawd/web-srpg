The unit: "Gatekeeper", the guardian of a starship's main reactor and the largest body of a rogue ship AI. Bipedal humanoid robot with no cockpit, towering and regal: a tall armored body with layered plates like ceremonial armor, a crown-like ring of small speaker pods around its smooth head, ONE large glowing red eye, long arms with blade-like fins on the forearms, and a heavy beam emitter built into the right forearm. Dark gunmetal with glowing teal and gold lines, and one faded white chest panel showing a worn emblem of a simple rising sun (no letters). Majestic, final.
