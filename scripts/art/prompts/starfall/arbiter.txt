The unit: "Arbiter", the personal command frame of a Directorate major. Bipedal humanoid mech, taller and more ornate than a trooper: white armor with crimson and gold trim, long armored skirt plates around the hips like a coat, tall shoulder pauldrons with gold edges, a crested helmet head with a single red visor line and a short commander's antenna. A long beam cannon built along the right forearm. Proud, severe, ceremonial.
