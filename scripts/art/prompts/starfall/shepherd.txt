The unit: "Shepherd", a command node of a rogue ship AI: a former tour-guide android that has grown into something larger. Bipedal humanoid robot with no cockpit, tall and thin with elongated limbs, long flat armor plates hanging from the waist like a robe, and a smooth oval head with ONE glowing red eye. A thin glowing teal ring is fixed to its upper back behind the head like a halo. Dark gunmetal and charcoal with teal light lines, and a few faded white panels with remnants of friendly orange signage stripes. A beam emitter built into the right forearm. Calm, eerie, priest-like.
