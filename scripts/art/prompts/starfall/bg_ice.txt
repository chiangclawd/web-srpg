Background painting for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9, seen from a high vantage point. A vast frozen plain on an ice moon at night: in the far distance, the colossal broken hull of a crashed generation starship lies half-buried in the ice, its ribs and decks exposed, a few tiny warm lights of settlements inside it. Above, a huge pale ringed gas giant fills a third of the starry sky, with a faint teal aurora. Drifting snow haze near the horizon.

Style: painted anime background, soft and atmospheric, LOW contrast and fairly dark overall (deep navy, slate blue, muted teal) so that bright game pieces placed on top stand out. The lower two thirds of the image must be calm, dark and almost empty (just dim ice and haze), with all the interesting detail in the upper third. No characters, no robots, no text, no UI, no frame.
