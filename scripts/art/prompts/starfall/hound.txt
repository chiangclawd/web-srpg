The unit: "Hound", a 4-metre salvage work-frame converted for combat, piloted by a young squad leader. Bipedal humanoid mech. White armor plates with safety-orange shoulder pads, knee guards and chest trim, a few black-and-yellow hazard stripes on the shins, dark graphite joints. Boxy cockpit torso with a wide cyan glowing visor slit; small head sensor unit with one short antenna. A short stubby assault rifle barrel is built into the right forearm; a small rectangular guard plate on the left forearm. A compact thruster pack on the back. Sturdy, friendly, heroic.
