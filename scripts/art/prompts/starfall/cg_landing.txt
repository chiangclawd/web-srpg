Story illustration for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9. Dusk on a frozen moon. View from inside a huge jagged breach in the hull of a wrecked starship, looking out over the frozen plain; the dark torn hull plating frames the left and right edges of the picture.

On the horizon a long line of military dropships is coming down to land, one after another, each with bright white landing lights and small crimson running lights: a whole row of lights from one side of the horizon to the other, searchlight beams sweeping the sky, steam and blown snow rising where the ships touch down. Above them the huge pale ringed gas giant and the last light of a cold orange sunset.

In the breach, against that light, exactly five defenders stand in one line, shoulder to shoulder. In the middle, seen from behind, the white-and-orange mech and the yellow mech look out at the horizon. On their left stand two identical gaunt dark gunmetal android machines, each with a single round eye; on their right, one six-legged spider-walker machine with a short mortar tube on its back. These three machines used to be enemies. They have turned to look back over their shoulders toward the viewer, so we see their eyes: the eyes and the thin light lines on their bodies glow a calm blue. Nothing on these three machines is red.

Mood: the calm before a siege; former enemies standing together.
