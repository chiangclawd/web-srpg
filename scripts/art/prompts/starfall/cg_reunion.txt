Story illustration for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9. Inside the cryo-sleep bay of a colossal wrecked starship: both walls are lined with upright sleep pods in long curving rows that recede into the dark like the sides of a canyon (the place shown in the attached background). The pods are opening: lids swung up, thick white mist pouring out and rolling across the floor, soft pale-cyan light everywhere.

In the centre, a gaunt man of 55 with warm brown skin, greying black hair and a short beard still rimed with frost, wearing a thin pale-blue cryo-suit, has just stepped out of an open pod. He is unsteady, one hand still on the rim of the pod. A lean young man of 22 holds him up with both hands: short messy black hair, warm brown skin, a patched white insulated pilot jacket with safety-orange shoulder panels, goggles pushed up on his forehead. The young man is seen from behind in three-quarter view, so we see mostly his back and a little of his cheek. The older man looks at the young man's face with a tired, amazed, gentle smile. They are father and son, the same two people as in the attached portraits.

Beside them hovers the small white egg-shaped drone with two round cyan eyes, watching. Far in the background, small, other sleepers are sitting up in their pods, and a big bald bearded man in a mustard-yellow coverall is helping one of them out.

Mood: relief; warmth in a cold place. A warm rim light on the two figures against the pale cyan.
