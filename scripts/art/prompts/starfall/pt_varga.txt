Character: Marshal Ilsa Varga, 63, supreme commander of the Directorate army. A tall, severe woman with silver-white hair pulled back in a tight low bun, a narrow lined face, pale blue eyes and a thin unsmiling mouth. She wears a black Directorate marshal's greatcoat with crimson lining, heavy gold braid on the shoulders, a high stiff collar and a single gold star pin. Expression: calm, absolute, a person who has never been told no.
