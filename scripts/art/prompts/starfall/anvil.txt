The unit: "Anvil", a 5-metre heavy dock-loader frame turned siege unit, piloted by a veteran foreman. Bipedal humanoid mech, very wide and squat with massive shoulders, huge forearms and heavy feet. Mustard-yellow armor with white panels and dark steel joints, a few black-and-yellow hazard stripes. A short fat cannon barrel is mounted on top of the right shoulder pointing forward. A thick rectangular shield slab is fixed to the left forearm. Small low head with one orange visor bar, two short exhaust stacks on the back. Tough, dependable, a walking wall.
