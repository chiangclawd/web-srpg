Title theme for a science-fiction tactics game set on a frozen moon, among the wreck of a huge colony starship.
Slow, lonely and beautiful ambient electronic piece: cold analog synth pads, a simple felt-piano motif of five notes that keeps returning, soft sub bass, distant glassy bell tones, a warm string swell in the second half. 72 BPM, D minor.
Instrumental only. Keep it gentle and even so it works as looping menu music.
