The unit: "Vesper", a slim ex-military sniper frame, piloted by a cool-headed deserter. Bipedal humanoid mech, taller and leaner than a work-frame, with angular sleek armor: deep navy blue with white panels and thin gold trim, dark joints. Narrow head with one round cyan sensor eye and a side scope lens. A long slender railgun barrel is built along the right forearm and extends past the hand. A small sensor pod on the left shoulder and two stabilizer fins on the back. Elegant, precise, cold.
