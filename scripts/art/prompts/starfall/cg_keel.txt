Story illustration for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9. Night on a frozen moon. A wrecked colony starship is pulling itself back together.

View from inside the ship along its main keel: an immense spine of dark girders with a wide deck running straight to a far vanishing point, pulsing with lines of teal light. The hull is torn open on both sides, and through the gaps we see the ice plain outside: colossal broken hull segments, each the size of a city block, are being dragged across the ice toward the ship by hundreds of taut cables and by swarms of tiny dark machines with teal lights, throwing up plumes of snow. Where a segment meets the ship, showers of welding sparks. Dust and frost shake loose from the girders overhead.

Small in the middle of the picture, seen from behind, a squad walks up the keel deck in formation: the white-and-orange mech in front, the yellow mech and the navy sniper mech on the flanks, a slim red scout mech at the rear, and in the middle of them the tiny white drone with its cyan glow.

Through the gaps in the hull: the huge pale ringed gas giant and a teal aurora.

Mood: awe; vast scale; everything in motion.
