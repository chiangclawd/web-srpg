The unit: "Swift", a light scout frame flown by a teenage courier. Bipedal humanoid mech, noticeably slimmer than a work-frame, with long legs and big rounded calf thrusters, a compact torso, and two short swept-back stabilizer wings on the back. Bright signal-red armor with white panels and dark graphite joints, one thin white racing stripe. Small rounded head with a wide yellow visor. A twin-barrel submachine gun built into the right forearm. Quick, cheeky, agile.
