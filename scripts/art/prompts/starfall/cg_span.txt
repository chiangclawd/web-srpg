Story illustration for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9. Night on a frozen moon.

The broken keel of a colossal starship, a single enormous girder of dark hull metal hundreds of metres long, lies across a bottomless chasm in the ice like a bridge. Its top is a narrow deck without railings, dusted with snow. Cold mist rises from the chasm, and a line of small teal emergency lights runs along the girder into the distance.

At the near end, seen from behind and small against the scale of the span: the white-and-orange mech in front, the yellow mech and the slim navy sniper mech just behind it, and the tiny white drone hovering beside them with its cyan glow. They are about to step onto the span.

At the far end stands a squat tram control station built from ship plating, glowing teal. In front of it one tall, thin, dark machine waits, with a single red eye and a thin teal ring behind its head like a halo. Around it and along the far half of the span, a dozen small red eye-lights of other machines in the dark.

Sky: the huge pale ringed gas giant and a faint teal aurora.

Mood: ominous and still; the only road forward.
