Story illustration for a sci-fi tactics game, 16:9. Deep under the ice of a frozen moon.

A junction station of an old tram line: a wide round tunnel bored through blue glacial ice and braced with ribs of salvaged starship plating, lit by a few cold work lamps. On the left, a boxy, patched tram car with warm yellow windows has stopped at the platform.

Just ahead of the tram the rails and the deck are cut clean through: a perfectly straight gap whose edges still glow dull orange, the rails ending in mid-air.

On the far side of the gap, a small, slim, bright signal-red scout mech with white panels, a wide yellow visor and two short swept-back wings on its back sprints toward us along the platform, its calf thrusters flaring. Close behind it bound three low four-legged dark gunmetal machines like robotic panthers, each with one narrow red eye slit and thin teal light lines.

In the right foreground, seen from behind, the white-and-orange mech raises its rifle toward the pursuers, with the yellow mech beside it.

Mood: sudden and kinetic; cold blue ice light against the warm tram windows and the red mech.
